# Social Studio backing music

Three selected tracks by Alejandro Magaña (A. M.), licensed through Mixkit for commercial social media videos, downloaded 27 October 2026. Track provenance, original and excerpt SHA-256 hashes, and licence URLs are recorded in manifest.json.

These server-only 12-second AAC excerpts support NovelForge’s own video exports. They are not a public music library. Do not move them into public/, offer standalone downloads, register them with Content ID, or claim ownership. The licence remains the creator’s and Mixkit’s; it is not the repository’s software licence.

Exports use a stable track choice per campaign, an 8-second animation for posters, and fades. Captions do not require music attribution under this licence. These are licensed backing tracks, not TikTok’s native trending sounds. For an automated rights claim, retain the saved manifest and refer to Mixkit’s licence and support process.

Review the linked terms before adding more tracks or using them in another product. No bulk downloading or music search runs at publish time.
