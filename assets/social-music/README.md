# Social Studio backing music

Three selected tracks by Alejandro Magaña (A. M.), licensed through Mixkit for commercial social media videos, downloaded 27 October 2026. Track provenance, original and excerpt SHA-256 hashes, and licence URLs are recorded in manifest.json.

These server-only 12-second AAC excerpts support NovelForge’s own video exports. They are not a public music library. Do not move them into public/, offer standalone downloads, register them with Content ID, or claim ownership. The licence remains the creator’s and Mixkit’s; it is not the repository’s software licence.

Exports use a stable track choice per campaign, an 8-second animation for posters, and fades. Captions do not require music attribution under this licence. These are licensed backing tracks, not TikTok’s native trending sounds. For an automated rights claim, retain the saved manifest and refer to Mixkit’s licence and support process.

Review the linked terms before adding more tracks or using them in another product. No bulk downloading or music search runs at publish time.

## Scene soundtracks, 26 November 2026

Fourteen additional excerpts are curated for this owner's romantic scene exports:
romantic/emotional, sensual/intimate, slow burn/tension and playful/flirty. The
three old tracks remain available only for existing selections and exports.
New automatic selections exclude those legacy clips.

Each new excerpt runs for 40 seconds, starting eight seconds into the original,
which covers the maximum 34-second scene sequence without repeating a short
loop. Excerpts are 48 kHz stereo AAC at 96 kbit/s, normalised to -18 LUFS with
a -2 dB true peak ceiling. The rendered video adds gentle entry/exit fades.
The private Studio previews the original source at the excerpt offset; bundled
audio is never exposed as a standalone download. Source and prepared hashes,
artist, catalogue and licence details are retained in manifest.json.

Scene selection uses on-screen copy to infer a default mood, or the owner's
chosen mood, and prefers tracks not used recently in their scene history.
The previewed ID is fixed in the durable render receipt, so retries keep that
soundtrack. Changing track reuses the stored photographs. New sequences default
to automatic music and scheduling; native platform music is an explicit manual
option. These tracks are backing music, not claims of platform trending status.
